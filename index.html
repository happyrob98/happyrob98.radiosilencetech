About RST

Born in the Deep

Radio Silence Technologies was founded by a U.S. Navy submarine veteran who served as a radioman in the Silent Service. The same discipline, precision, and mission focus that defined years of submarine operations now drives every engagement we undertake.

We are a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) providing technology and engineering services to government and commercial clients.

When the mission demands reliability, you need a team that has operated under pressure — literally.
